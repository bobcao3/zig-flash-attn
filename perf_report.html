Inter-sequence attention kernels

Inter-sequence attention kernels pack many short sequences of different lengths end to end on one token axis, and a visibility rule lets each query sequence attend across sequence boundaries to the keys of every sequence it may see. On short-sequence varlen batches they beat cuDNN and FA4 by a wide margin; each figure below shows one technique behind that.
